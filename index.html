Authentic Farm Process

From Field to Lawn — Freshly Harvested

We do not keep sod sitting in dusty warehouses. See how our real harvesting and delivery cycle guarantees lush, living grass ready to root in your soil.

01

Field Cultivation

Turf is nurtured on rich farm soil with balanced organic feed and deep irrigation until a dense, interconnected root mat is established.

02

Precision Sod Cutting

On the morning of your order, our sod-cutting machine slices the turf with uniform root-depth into neat 10, 20, 30, or 40 sq ft rolls.

03

Hand-Rolling & Loading

Our experienced team inspects, rolls, and stacks the turf rolls onto transport vehicles to protect moisture and avoid heat damage.

04

Laying & Rooting

Delivered straight to your site and laid seamlessly edge-to-edge. Watered immediately to settle roots into your ground within days.

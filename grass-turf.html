How to Care for Freshly Laid Sod

  • Water Immediately: Saturate the grass within 30 minutes of laying so moisture reaches the soil underneath.
  • Daily Watering: Water twice daily (early morning and late afternoon) for the first 10-14 days until roots anchor firmly.
  • First Mowing: Wait approximately 12–14 days before the first gentle trim. Never cut more than 1/3 of the grass blade.
  • Professional Laying Support: Need our expert team to level, fertilize, and lay your turf? Call us at 7398869340.
